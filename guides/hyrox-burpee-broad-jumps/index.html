HYROX station 4

Burpee broad jumps
Strength doesn't win here. Rhythm does.

Station four and, for many people, the worst one. No equipment, just you, the floor and 80 metres after both sleds. Fly through the first twenty metres and you will be lying on the floor at halfway.

Start now

HYROX station 4

Rules

  • 80 m in every division, bodyweight only.
  • Your chest touches the floor on every burpee.
  • Your hands go down no more than one foot length in front of your toes.
  • Take-off and landing are two-footed, feet side by side. No steps forward between landing and the next burpee.

Check the current HYROX rulebook before race day.

HYROX station 4

Technique: the same rep every time

  • Down: hands just in front of your toes, feet back, chest on the floor. Don't crash, don't brake with your arms.
  • Up: stepping up is slower but saves heart rate. Jumping up is faster and costs more. Test both after 40 m.
  • Jump forward, not up, at about 70–80% of your max. Land softly on your whole foot.
  • Breathe: out on the way down, in on the way up. If you must rest, rest standing.

HYROX station 4

Common mistakes

  • Max-effort jumps: every jump is a show, after 30 m you have nothing left.
  • Hands too far forward or a one-foot take-off: risk of no-reps.
  • Lying on the floor between reps. The seconds pile up.
  • Sprinting the start: the second half takes twice as long.

HYROX station 4

Pacing: count jumps, not metres

Do 80 m fresh at a pace you can hold and count your jumps. 44 jumps means 11 for every 20 m. On race day you count to eleven, four times, and ignore how far is left.

After the burpees your heart rate is the highest of the race. Run the first 200 m a little slower. The good news: the next station is the row, where you sit down.

HYROX station 4

Workouts

  1. Race rhythm: 4 × 40 m at race rhythm, 90 s rest. Same jump count every rep.
  2. 10-min EMOM: 6–8 burpee broad jumps every minute.
  3. Station four: 1 km run + 80 m burpee broad jumps + 1 km run, no walking.
  4. After the sled: 3 rounds of 25 m sled pull + 400 m run + 30 m burpee broad jumps + 400 m run.

FAQ

Common questions.

How far are the burpee broad jumps in HYROX?

80 m in every division. It is station four, after the sled push and sled pull.

What are the rules for HYROX burpee broad jumps?

Chest to the floor on every rep, hands no further than one foot length in front of your toes, and a two-footed take-off and landing with no steps forward between reps.

Should I step or jump up in the burpees?

Test both in training. Stepping up is slower per rep but keeps your heart rate lower, and many athletes are faster overall with it.

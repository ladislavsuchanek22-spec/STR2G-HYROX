How we train

Eight kilometres. Eight stations. No guesswork.

Anyone can write a hard workout. A good plan gets you to the start line ready and over the finish line without leaking time. Every STR2G program is built on the same five things.

Running

Half the race. Pace, economy and running that holds up after the sleds.

Strength

A stronger athlete moves the sled faster and leaves the station fresher.

Stations

Technique that saves seconds and energy at all eight stations.

Fatigue

Compromised running and work under fatigue. The stuff that decides race day.

Head

Tactics, pacing and holding your output when it starts to hurt.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need HYROX experience?

No. If you're starting out, go with HYROX Basic. It builds your base and teaches you the stations. Competitor, Pro Athlete and the race builds are for people already training consistently.

Can beginners follow the programs?

Yes. You scale loads and volume to your level. The goal is to finish each session with quality, not just survive it.

Do you have a program for Doubles?

Yes, the 8-Week HYROX Doubles build. It covers splitting the work, station strategy and shared pacing. The other programs work for Doubles too, since you both still need to run well under fatigue.

Program or 1:1 coaching?

A program is one plan shared by everyone on it, with scaling options. 1:1 coaching is written only for you around your performance, recovery and schedule, and adjusted as you go. If you have a specific goal or limited time, 1:1 gets you there faster.

12-week or 8-week race build?

If you have the time, take 12 weeks. You get a proper base before the intensity. Eight weeks is for athletes who already train regularly and want to sharpen up for a race.

What equipment do I need?

Ideally a gym with ergs and HYROX equipment. Missing something? Message us on Instagram and we'll suggest a swap.

How do I cancel a monthly subscription?

You manage your subscription directly in Strivee. Race builds and specialist blocks are a one-time payment.

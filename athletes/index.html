05 · FAQ

Common questions.

Is this a training program?

No. It's the team. You get a weekly workout and know-how. Full plans stay in the Strivee app and annual members get 15 % off them.

How do I get the 15 % discount?

Go annual, log in to the members area and your codes are there: one for personal training, one for programs in Strivee.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime in the members area. Monthly stops at the end of the paid month. Annual runs until the end of the year you paid for.

Do I need to train in a STR2G club?

No. Train wherever you are. Members in Prague, Brno and Ostrava can also meet us in the clubs.

EUR or CZK?

Both. Checkout shows CZK in the Czech Republic and EUR everywhere else.
